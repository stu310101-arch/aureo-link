AUREO LINK free mode includes the official cloudflared binary from Cloudflare.
Project: https://github.com/cloudflare/cloudflared
License: Apache License 2.0; see tools/LICENSE-cloudflared.txt.
Release URL and official SHA256 are recorded in tools/cloudflared-release.json.
The binary is distributed unchanged and verified during packaging.

Cloudflare Quick Tunnels provide temporary HTTPS access to the local AUREO relay.
No Cloudflare account or purchased domain is required for this mode.
These temporary tunnels have no uptime guarantee and change hostname on restart.
Documentation: https://developers.cloudflare.com/tunnel/get-started/quick-tunnels/

The executable also bundles Python, Tcl/Tk, aiohttp, Pillow, qrcode,
websocket-client and their runtime dependencies. Their upstream licenses apply.
Exact package versions are recorded in requirements-build-lock.txt.

The Windows setup wizard is built with Inno Setup 6.7.3.
Project: https://jrsoftware.org/ and https://github.com/jrsoftware/issrc
Its runtime license is installed under licenses/InnoSetup/LICENSE.txt.
The compiler is fetched from the official release and verified against SHA256.
